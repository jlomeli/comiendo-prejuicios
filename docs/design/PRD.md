# Project PRD: Comiendo Prejuicios (Luz de Atardecer)

## 1. Project Overview

"Comiendo Prejuicios" is a digital sanctuary designed for women navigating eating disorders, trauma, and neurodivergence. This specific version, **Luz de Atardecer (Sunset Light)**, prioritizes emotional safety and a sense of being "held" through a warm, editorial aesthetic.

**Primary Goal:** To provide a low-barrier, non-clinical entry point for Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) that feels "Safe First, Competent Second."

  - **Surface:** `#FBF9F5` (Oat / Warm Cream) - The primary background color to avoid clinical white.

- **Typography:**
  - **Headlines:** *Noto Serif* (Italic, Light weights) - Humanist, soft, and sophisticated.
  - **Body:** *Be Vietnam Pro* or *Newsreader* - High readability for neurodivergent and anxious readers.

### Motion Philosophy: "The Breath"

- **Dynamics:** 400–700ms transitions mimicking a slow breathing rhythm.
- **Implementation:** Scroll-triggered fade-and-drift upward entries (IntersectionObserver).
- **Accessibility:** Strict adherence to `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 3. Screen Architecture: Landing Page

The landing page is a comprehensive, single-page journey designed to build trust.

### 3.1. Hero Section

- **Headline:** "Un camino hacia la paz contigo misma" (A path toward peace with yourself).
- **Sub-headline:** Focuses on psychological accompaniment from ACT and gender perspectives.
- **Visuals:** Organic shapes and warm, natural photography (plants/sunlight).

### 3.2. Identification Section

- **"¿Te sientes identificada?"**: Four cards addressing common pain points (eating as coping, hostil body image, autocriticism, and the "thinness trap").

### 3.3. Personal Connection

- **"Hola, soy Ana"**: A section introducing the therapist to humanize the clinical aspect. Includes professional experience metrics (8+ years, 500+ lives).

### 3.4. The ACT Approach

- **Core Message:** "No se trata de arreglarte. Se trata de tu libertad." (It's not about fixing you. It's about your freedom.)
- **Hexaflex Simplification:** Three cards explaining Acceptance, Mindfulness, and Values in jargon-free language.

### 3.5. The Process

- **Four-Step Timeline:** Reassuring path from the initial 20-min free evaluation to ongoing path sessions.

### 3.6. Investment &amp; Social Proof

- **Transparent Pricing:** Clear cards for individual sessions and discounted 4-session bundles.
- **Testimonials:** "Voces del camino" carousel featuring empathetic patient reflections.

### 3.7. Conversion &amp; Engagement

- **Contact Form:** Minimal, high-contrast container for direct inquiries.
- **Newsletter:** "¿Deseas recibir calma en tu correo?" - Low-pressure lead capture positioned just above the footer.

## 4. Technical Specifications

- **Framework:** Tailwind CSS for responsive layout and semantic utility-first styling.
- **Language:** 100% Spanish (Castilian/Neutral).
- **Accessibility:**
  - WCAG AA contrast ratios (Standardized across the Sunset palette).
  - Responsive breakpoints: 375px (Mobile), 768px (Tablet), 1280px (Desktop).
  - High-visibility focus states for neurodivergent navigation.
- **SEO/Social:** Optimized for search terms related to "Terapia ACT," "Trastornos Alimentarios," and "Perspectiva de Género."

---

## 5. Success Metrics

- **Emotional Safety:** Measured by qualitative user feedback on the "vibe" of the page.
- **Lead Quality:** Conversion rate for the 20-minute free evaluation vs. total traffic.
- **Educational Value:** Average scroll depth and interaction with the ACT Hexaflex section.
